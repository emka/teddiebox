# Code of conduct

- Be respectful. Argue about the code, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding someone is wrong.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic.

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules,
and may block those who keep breaking them.

To report a problem privately, use the **Security** tab of the repository,
then **Report a vulnerability**.
